About

I work at the intersection of digital investigations, digital forensics, cybersecurity, and AI security, with particular attention to the operational handling of digital evidence and the reliability of technologies used in investigative workflows.

Professional Profile

My work combines operational practice, technical development, and applied research. It spans forensic workflows, cybersecurity and incident support, OSINT, external exposure analysis, and the evaluation of AI-assisted investigative tools.

I am particularly interested in decisions that affect evidence preservation, traceability, repeatability, and defensibility — especially when systems are live, remote, cloud-connected, or AI-assisted.

Academic Work and Research

I am completing a Master's degree in Computer Engineering, Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Cagliari.

My current research examines the operational robustness of AI-based forensic tools under clean, adversarial, anti-forensic, and out-of-distribution conditions, with emphasis on reproducibility, traceable datasets, human-in-the-loop validation, and failure analysis.

Evidence First

Evidence First is an independently developed series of digital-evidence field guides focused on state-driven operational decisions. The method starts from the observable state, compares realistic alternatives, and makes gain, loss, footprint, risk, reversibility, decision, and documentation explicit.

The project grew from a recurring field problem: an intervention can preserve evidence or retain access, but it can also consume options that may not be recoverable later.
