Short bio

Biography

Lello Molinario works across digital investigations, digital forensics, cybersecurity and AI security. His work focuses on digital-evidence handling, forensic acquisition and the reliability of AI-assisted investigative workflows. He is the author of Evidence First and develops the FAIR-Lab forensic AI robustness framework.

Publication

Evidence First

Evidence First — Digital Evidence Field Guides is an independent, decision-oriented publication series. The flagship is Vol. 1: Computer — Computer First Response & Forensic Acquisition, 2027 Edition.
