02 / Method

A methodology built around accountability.

Whether working with forensic procedures or AI-assisted analysis, the same priority applies: understand what the system changes, what it preserves and what it may fail to reveal.

01 / PRESERVE

Know what may be lost.

Identify volatile information, irreversible actions and competing acquisition priorities before acting.

02 / VALIDATE

Test what can fail.

Examine assumptions, robustness and limitations instead of treating automated outputs as definitive findings.

03 / EXPLAIN

Make decisions reviewable.

Document reasoning, uncertainties and technical choices so the work remains understandable beyond the tool.

A connected approach across forensic practice, research and engineering.
