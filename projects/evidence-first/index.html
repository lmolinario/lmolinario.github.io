Why I built it

Digital forensics guidance often explains principles, artifacts, or tool capabilities. The recurring operational problem is different: an examiner or first responder is facing a concrete evidence state and must decide what to do next. Evidence First is designed around that decision boundary rather than around a product, command set, or artifact catalog.

The Evidence First method

Each operational decision is evaluated through the same fixed sequence, so the reader can compare what may be preserved, what may be lost, what footprint is introduced, what risks follow, and whether the choice is realistically reversible before acting.

What distinguishes the series

State-driven

Start from observable device and evidence state before intervention, not from a preferred forensic tool.

Consequence-first

Compare realistic actions through gain, loss, footprint, risk, and reversibility before selecting the next step.

Tool-independent

Keep the decision logic useful as platforms, interfaces, products, and acquisition capabilities change.

Series scope

The canonical architecture separates Computer, Mobile Devices, Network & Remote Infrastructure, Cloud & SaaS, Embedded & IoT Devices, and Complex Scenes. Each local decision has one owning volume; cross-domain sequencing belongs to Complex Scenes. The official Evidence First site contains the maintained series description and the current publication surface.
